# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Coaches and log analysts who review many Warcraft Logs reports, often from different guilds, in
long sessions. The panel is docked next to WCL's dark analysis page while they work. They build a
library of pins, sets and saved views (tab, Friendlies/Enemies, ability, layout) and re-apply them
to report after report. They are power users: they know WCL's vocabulary, value speed and density,
and notice when a tool does something other than what its label says.

## Product Purpose

Opposite Pin Library saves, organizes and re-applies WCL pins and report views, because WCL has no
native pin library. It reads the active report, lets the analyst save pins with tags and views,
groups them into sets, and applies them to any report in one step through WCL's own URL routing.
Success: an analyst opens a new report and has a known analysis setup on screen in seconds, and
trusts that it shows exactly what the label promised.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, trustworthy. The voice is terse and factual and uses WCL's own names (Damage Taken,
Debuffs, Friendlies, Enemies) instead of inventing terms. The emotional goal is confidence and
flow: the tool disappears and the analysis keeps moving.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS or Bootstrap: stacks of rounded cards, default blue buttons, template feel.
- Fantasy or WoW kitsch: parchment, ornate frames, game-UI skeuomorphism.
- Neon gamer: glows, saturated gradients, RGB aesthetics.
- WCL-style clutter: dense walls of tiny controls with weak hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. The report is the hero. The panel supports WCL's charts and never competes with them for
   attention.
2. Density with hierarchy. Show a lot at once, but give every item one obvious primary action.
3. Labels mirror WCL exactly, so what the analyst reads is what gets applied.
4. Built for long sessions: fast to scan, fully keyboard-operable, no needless confirmation steps.
5. Color carries meaning (pin kinds, status, the active view), never decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: AA contrast for text and controls in both themes, full keyboard operation, visible
focus, color never the only carrier of meaning (pin kinds always have a text label), and
`prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
